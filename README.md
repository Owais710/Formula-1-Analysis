# 🏎️ Formula 1 Season Dashboard

An interactive **Formula 1 analytics dashboard** that visualizes the 2025 season with insights into race weekends, championship standings, car performance, and strategy decisions.

This project transforms raw race and season data into clear, modern charts so fans, analysts, and engineers can quickly understand trends and performance.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

### 🗓 Weekend Format
Quick summary of a typical race weekend:
- **Friday:** Practice 1 & 2 – data gathering and setup
- **Saturday:** Practice 3 + Qualifying – grid position decided
- **Sunday:** Race – points awarded

---

### ⚖️ Car Weight Distribution
Pie chart showing how total car weight is split between:
- Chassis
- Engine & Gearbox
- Suspension
- Fuel load

Helps illustrate regulation limits and engineering trade-offs.

---

### 🏆 Drivers Championship
Bar chart comparing:
- Driver points
- Season standings
- Performance gaps

Quickly highlights title contenders and midfield battles.

---

### 🏁 Constructors Championship
Line/point chart showing:
- Team rankings
- Total points
- Competitive gaps between teams

Useful for tracking development progress over the season.

---

### 🌀 Downforce Contribution
Bar chart breaking down aerodynamic performance from:
- Floor & diffuser
- Front wing
- Rear wing
- Other components

Shows which areas contribute most to overall grip.

---

### 🛞 Tire Strategy
Donut chart visualizing compound usage:
- Soft
- Medium
- Hard

Helps analyze race strategy and stint planning.
